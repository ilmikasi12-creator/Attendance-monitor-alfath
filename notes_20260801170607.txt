Build a complete Android application called Pondok Attendance Monitoring System for an Islamic boarding school (pesantren). This application is specifically designed for the Attendance Team Meeting (Musyawarah Tim Absen) process and daily student attendance monitoring.

The application must feel like a professional monitoring dashboard, similar to a control center or analytics system, not a typical school attendance app.

Main Objective

The system collects attendance reports from multiple departments (Instansi), automatically calculates Alpha (unexcused absence) statistics, identifies repeated absences, and provides dashboards and reports for daily, weekly, and monthly evaluation meetings.

User Roles

1. Department Officer (Instansi Officer)

Departments:

- Diniyyah
- Tahfidz
- Qoilulah
- Ngasor
- Manzil
- Mutola'ah
- Hafalan Kitab
- Night Attendance
- Complex

Permissions:

- Login using their own account.
- Submit attendance reports only for their assigned department.
- View only their department's data and history.
- View department-specific graphs and statistics.
- Cannot access or edit any other department.

2. Attendance Coordinator

Permissions:

- View all departments.
- View total Alpha cases across the entire boarding school.
- View real-time dashboards.
- View daily, weekly, and monthly trends.
- Compare departments.
- View students with repeated Alpha records.
- Update follow-up status.

3. Principal / Senior Ustadz

Permissions:

- Read-only access.
- View dashboards, graphs, reports, and evaluations.
- Cannot modify data.
